About this compilation

The only question paper IIM publishes is the candidate response sheet — a page showing one candidate's exam with the correct answers marked. 398 of these questions are built directly from those files. The other 214 are from slots IIM never published a sheet for, and are marked via cracku wherever they appear; see below.

Why many questions are pictures

The response sheet publishes math-typeset questions as images rather than text. Those are embedded here exactly as published, so for them no transcription step exists and no transcription error is possible. Questions written in plain language are published as text, and that text is used verbatim.

Why there are no question numbers

CAT shows every candidate a different question and option order. A response sheet's "Q7" is that candidate's seventh question, not the paper's. Questions are therefore labelled by year and slot only. The option letters are the order shown here, and the answer key matches them.

If you compare against another site, check the answer's value, not its letter — their option order comes from the original paper and will often differ.

Two sources, always labelled

398 questions come from official IIM response sheets. The other 214 are from slots IIM never published a sheet for — CAT 2017 and 2018 predate the format, and three later sheets were never indexed publicly. Those are taken from Cracku's published copy and are marked via cracku on every page they appear on.

Mirror questions are someone else's transcription of the paper, so they carry that site's typesetting and its answer rather than an official key. They are included for completeness and labelled so you always know which you are looking at.

Answers

596 of 612 questions carry an answer. The remaining 16 are mostly CAT 2025 questions whose official sheet prints Possible Answer: NA; those show a dash rather than a guess.

Worked solutions

The answer keys link to a published worked solution where one exists, but do not reproduce it — those solutions are their authors' work. Follow the link in any answer key to read one.
